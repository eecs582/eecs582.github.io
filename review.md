## Paper Review

This class requires extensive readings of systems research papers. To help you with this and to ensure a productive discussion in class, you are asked to write a brief review for the required reading <b>before</b> the class, 
and submit the review through [Gradescope](https://www.gradescope.com).

We provided a [template](eecs582_review_summary.md) to guide the review writing. However, you do not have to follow that template. Regardless of the format, the review should summarize the key points and **your** thoughts. Be concise. Try to keep it within one page.

**If a paper has listed questions (on the [Schedule](schedule.html) page), your review is only required to answer the listed questions (i.e., a summary can be omitted).**

If there are two or more required papers in one lecture, you are required to write a review for at least *one paper*. However, if both papers have listed questions, your review needs to answer all the questions. Note this choice only applies to the review---you must still **read all** the required papers.

You are also encouraged to include in your review any questions you would like to see discussed 
in class.
